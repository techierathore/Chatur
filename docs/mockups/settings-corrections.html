Why Chatur changed it
"Four of the requirements I wrote for TfLens had no test attached, so the Verifier had nothing to check and passed them on the build alone. I have made the test part of writing the requirement, and I stop and ask when I cannot write one."
Seen in Session of 22 September, 10:48 — "Phase 2 requirements"
A correction you keep goes into the next build's seed data, so Chatur starts there rather than learning it again. A correction you undo does not — the wording it replaced comes back and the record stays here for the reading.
